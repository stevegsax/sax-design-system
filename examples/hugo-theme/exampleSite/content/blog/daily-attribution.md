Monthly attribution tells you what happened. Daily attribution tells you when it
happened, which is the difference between a story and a diagnosis.

<!--more-->

## Timing is the signal

Most of the value in attribution shows up in the first week of a regime change.
A monthly report blends that week into three others and reports the average, which
is exactly the information you needed and exactly the information it discards.

With a daily series you can see whether a drawdown was a single bad session or a
slow bleed, whether a hedge engaged on the day it was supposed to, and whether two
strategies that look uncorrelated monthly are in fact moving together intraday.

## Eat your own cooking

We publish attribution to clients on the same cadence we consume it internally.
If a report is not good enough to trade on, it is not good enough to send. That
constraint keeps the reporting honest: there is no separate, prettier deck for
outside consumption.
